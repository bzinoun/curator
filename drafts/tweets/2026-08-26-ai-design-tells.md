Gradient text. A colored bar above every heading. A decorative border down the side of each card.

Ran my UI through a 60-detector audit and got flagged on all three. Not because they're ugly — because they're what AI-generated design does by default.

The lesson isn't "delete them." It's: know the tells. Then a side border is a choice, not a habit.
