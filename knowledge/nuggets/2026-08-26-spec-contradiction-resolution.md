When two official sources disagree—design mock vs. CR from two days ago—**don't guess which one holds**. Instead:

1. **Document both** in code comments and PR descriptions, named by source and date.
2. **Surface explicitly** in the working session (note, presentation, ticket).
3. **Gate the decision** to the synchronous meeting where arbitrage is live (workshop, standup, design review).

Here, the Figma MVP showed 2 tabs; the 18 Aug CR asked for 3 blocks. Suppressing either one silently means half the stakeholders get surprised later.
