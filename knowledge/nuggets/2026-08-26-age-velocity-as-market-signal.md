App store etagères reveal fundamentally different opportunities via a single, overlooked metric: **launch velocity** (reviews per year). Same median review count masks opposite realities:

- **Old + slow** (e.g., 8.1 years, 83 reviews/year): dormant dominants. The incumbents captured the niche then abandoned active development — attaquable.
- **Young + slow** (e.g., 1.4 years, 7 reviews/year): *tried and failed*. Six recent apps entered, none gained traction. Same low review count, but it's a graveyard, not an opportunity.

Measured on 40 real niches: "posture reminder" (young+slow) dropped from rank 1 to rank 36 when velocity was introduced. The insight holds across all app categories: **never rank opportunities by raw metrics alone; pair them with temporal depth.** One faux positif would send builders into a category where six predecessors already failed.
