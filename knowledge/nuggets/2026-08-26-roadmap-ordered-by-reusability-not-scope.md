A multi-domain roadmap became credible by ordering new branches by how much existing machinery they reuse, not by market size or engineering effort alone.

Core insight: one engine (read documents → identify parties → establish liability → apply barème) serves multiple domains. The roadmap should sequence by leverage:

1. **Strengthen the engine** (multi-document accumulation, mixed-domain claims)
2. **Extend via reuse** (material damage: same PV, liability already calculated, just a different rate table)
3. **Introduce new branches** (workplace accident: new legal regime, but existing portals and verification reused)

This transforms "we're building AT separately" to "AT proves our core architecture is sound." It attracts different investors and engineers—people betting on a platform, not a point product.
